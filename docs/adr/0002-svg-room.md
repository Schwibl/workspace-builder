# The room is one SVG, not catalog photos

Product photos would look specific and would also freeze the theme: a daylight JPEG cannot become a moon. They would also depend on monis.rent image URLs we do not control.

The room is an original elevation in `room-scene.tsx`. Desk width, chair back, monitors, lamp, and plant are drawn from the setup. The window's day and night skies are both in the SVG and the active theme only changes opacity, so the switch does not wait on a new asset.
