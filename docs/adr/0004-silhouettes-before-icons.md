# Empty slots stay visible as silhouettes

Hiding a monitor until a desk exists made the room look simpler and also hid a choice the renter had already made. The room now always shows five slots. An empty slot is a dashed silhouette with a plus that opens that category. A chosen slot is the explicit icon, and a monitor, lamp, or plant may be an icon while the desk is still empty.

Desk and chair stay required for a rental request. They clear only with Start over.
