# monis.rent workspace designer

A freelancer lands in Bali and needs a desk by next week. This app lets them build that room on one screen — desk, chair, monitor, lamp, plant — see it update, and request the rental.

The room is the interface. Each category is a single choice. The window above the desk is daylight in the white theme and a moon in the dark theme. Checkout is the only second screen: term, Bali area, name, email. Rent places a request. It does not charge a card.

Language for these words lives in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md).
