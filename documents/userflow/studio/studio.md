# Studio

1. The renter opens `/` and sees the room. Empty slots are dashed silhouettes with a plus.
2. Hover stretches a piece and pulses it. A chosen piece pulses on its own. Click opens that category in the sidebar when the list is open, or a pop-up beside the piece when the sidebar is collapsed. The option list scrolls without a visible scrollbar.
3. A radio choice replaces that silhouette with an explicit icon. Hovering an option that is not selected fills that row with the page paper and a side rail. The selected row keeps its mint fill. "None" on monitor, lamp, or plant returns the silhouette.
4. Monitor, lamp, and plant can be chosen before a desk. The other optional slots stay open.
5. White theme shows daylight in the window. Dark theme shows the moon.
6. Review rental goes to `/checkout`. Rent needs a desk and a chair. Start over clears the setup.
