# Studio — UX

The room is the control. An empty slot shows where a piece goes. A chosen piece is the piece.

## Path

1. Land on the room. The window is large. Five slots are already drawn as empty silhouettes: desk, chair, monitor, lamp, plant. Each silhouette has one circle with a plus.
2. Tap a plus. That category opens beside the room. One radio fills the slot. The silhouette becomes the explicit icon immediately.
3. Monitor, lamp, and plant are optional. They can be chosen and they appear even when the desk is still empty. Choosing one does not lock the others.
4. Desk and chair are the only pieces required to rent. They have no "none" row. Optional pieces do, so the icon can return to a silhouette.
5. The bar under the room shows the weekly total and **Review rental**.
6. **Start over** clears every slot back to a silhouette.
7. White / Dark changes the app and the window together: daylight, or the moon.

## What we refuse to add

- Drag and drop. The plus is one hit, then one radio.
- Hiding optional pieces until a desk exists. That adds a wait the renter did not ask for.
- A second control that only changes the sky.

## Empty

Nothing chosen: five silhouettes, five pluses, total $0. The caption names the sky and says the slots are open. Review still opens.

## Loading

Until the saved setup is read, the page says "Opening the room…" and does not flash empty silhouettes over a saved room.
