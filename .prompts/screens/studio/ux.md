# Studio — UX

The room is the control. An empty slot shows where a piece goes. A chosen piece is the piece. The window draws the time of day. A sentence under the picture does not.

## Path

1. Land on the room. Five slots are already drawn: desk, chair, monitor, lamp, plant. Each empty slot is a silhouette with one circle and a plus. The weekly price sits where it can be seen with the room.
2. Hover a piece. It answers, so the slot feels clickable before the click.
3. Click a piece while the sidebar is open. That category opens in the sidebar. One radio fills the slot. The silhouette becomes the explicit icon immediately.
4. Collapse the sidebar. The list leaves. Click a piece and the options for that slot open in a pop-up beside that piece. The same radios. One choice, and the drawing updates.
5. Expand the sidebar and the pop-up closes. Clicks go back to the sidebar tabs.
6. Monitor, lamp, and plant are optional. They can be chosen and they appear even when the desk is still empty. Choosing one does not lock the others.
7. Desk and chair are the only pieces required to rent. They have no "none" row. Optional pieces do, so the icon can return to a silhouette.
8. The bar under the room shows the weekly total and **Review rental**. On a laptop-height screen that bar is in view with the open sidebar. No page scroll.
9. **Start over** clears every slot back to a silhouette.
10. White / Dark changes the app and the window together: daylight, or the moon. Nothing under the picture repeats that.

## What we refuse to add

- Drag and drop. The piece is one hit, then one radio.
- Hiding optional pieces until a desk exists.
- A caption that narrates the sky.
- A second desktop layout. The phone may stack. It does not get its own arrangement of columns.

## Empty

Nothing chosen: five silhouettes, five pluses, total $0. Review still opens.

## Loading

Until the saved setup is read, the page says "Opening the room…" and does not flash empty silhouettes over a saved room.
