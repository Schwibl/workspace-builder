# Studio — UX

A freelancer in Bali should see a room, change one thing, and watch the room change. No catalog pages. No wizard.

## Path

1. Land on an empty room: floor, rug, and a window. Nothing else.
2. Categories sit beside the room (under it on a phone): Desk, Chair, Monitor, Lamp, Plant. Desk is open.
3. One choice per category. Choosing it updates the drawing immediately.
4. Desk and chair have no "none" row. Accessories do, so they can be removed without a second control.
5. A bar under the room shows the weekly total and **Review rental**.
6. **Start over** appears only after something is selected.
7. White / Dark in the header changes the whole app and the window (sun or moon) in the same click.

## What we refuse to add

- Drag and drop. Pointing at a card is one action; dragging is more.
- A height slider. The three desks already differ in width and mechanism.
- A cart of duplicate plants or monitors. Dual screens are one option.

## Empty

No desk, no chair, no accessories: the caption says the room is empty and to pick a desk. The total is $0. Review still opens, so the empty checkout is one click away.

## Loading

Until the saved setup is read, the page says "Opening the room…" and does not flash an empty room over a saved one.
